# Bookkeeper OS

Invoicing & Bookkeeping for small businesses in the US, on Mac and Windows. By OS Factory.

This repository holds only the finished program files (under "Releases"), no source code.
The latest version is always here: https://osfactory.app/bookkeeper-os/

- Mac: macOS 13 or later, Intel and Apple silicon, signed and notarized by Apple
- Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64 and ARM64)

Your books stay on your computer. The app connects only to check your license and to look for new versions.
